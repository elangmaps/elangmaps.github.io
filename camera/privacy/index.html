Datria GPS Camera – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Datria GPS Camera ("the app") is developed and published by Damatria Developer, an individual developer. Contact: damatria.dev@gmail.com.

Summary

Datria GPS Camera takes photos stamped with the date, time, and location. Photos and settings stay on your device. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. The developer never receives your photos or location.

Data the app uses

DataWhy it is usedWhere it is stored
CameraTo take photos. The app does not record audio or videoPhotos are saved on the device
Precise location (GPS)To print coordinates, altitude, accuracy, and direction on the photo stamp and in the photo's metadata (EXIF). Location is used only while the app is openOn the device only, inside the photos you take
PhotosStamped photos are saved to Pictures/Datria GPS Camera; if you turn on "keep original", an unstamped copy is saved in its Asli subfolder. Photos contain location metadata (EXIF), and other apps with photo access can read themIn your device gallery
Text and images you add (notes, project name, the logo image you pick for the stamp) and your settingsTo print on the stamp and remember your choicesIn the app's private storage on the device

Network connections

The app makes no connection on its own except the ones listed here. Neither of them receives your photos.

  • Address on the stamp. If the address line is on, the app asks Android's built-in Geocoder to turn your coordinates into a street address. On most phones this service is provided by Google, which receives the coordinates under Google's privacy policy. Turn off the address line to avoid this.
  • Mini map on the stamp. If the map is on, the app downloads street-map tiles for the area around you from OpenFreeMap (data © OpenStreetMap contributors). The request reveals your IP address and the approximate map area, as with any web map. Turn off the map to avoid this.

Sharing

The app never shares photos on its own. When you tap Share, the Android share menu opens and you choose where the photo goes. Shared photos keep their stamp and location metadata, so share them only with people you trust.

Retention and deletion

Photos stay in your gallery until you delete them, in the app or in any gallery app. Deleting a stamped photo in the app also deletes its original copy with the same name. Uninstalling the app removes its settings but keeps photos already saved in your gallery. Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing to request deletion of on our side.

Children

The app is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: damatria.dev@gmail.com.

Datria GPS Camera – Kebijakan Privasi (Bahasa Indonesia)

Terakhir diperbarui: 14 November 2026

Datria GPS Camera ("aplikasi") dikembangkan dan diterbitkan oleh Damatria Developer, pengembang perorangan. Kontak: damatria.dev@gmail.com.

Datria GPS Camera mengambil foto bercap tanggal, jam, dan lokasi. Foto dan pengaturan tetap di HP Anda. Tidak ada akun, iklan, analitik, atau pelacakan. Pengembang tidak pernah menerima foto atau lokasi Anda.

Data yang dipakai aplikasi

  • Kamera: untuk mengambil foto. Aplikasi tidak merekam suara atau video.
  • Lokasi akurat (GPS): untuk mencetak koordinat, ketinggian, akurasi, dan arah pada cap foto serta metadata foto (EXIF). Lokasi hanya dipakai saat aplikasi terbuka.
  • Foto: foto bercap disimpan di Pictures/Datria GPS Camera; bila "simpan asli" dinyalakan, salinan tanpa cap disimpan di subfolder Asli. Foto memuat metadata lokasi (EXIF) dan bisa dibaca aplikasi lain yang punya akses foto.
  • Teks dan gambar yang Anda tambahkan (catatan, nama proyek, gambar logo cap) dan pengaturan: disimpan di penyimpanan privat aplikasi di HP.

Koneksi jaringan

Aplikasi tidak terhubung ke internet kecuali untuk hal berikut. Tidak satu pun menerima foto Anda.

  • Alamat pada cap: bila baris alamat dinyalakan, aplikasi meminta Geocoder bawaan Android mengubah koordinat menjadi alamat. Di kebanyakan HP layanan ini disediakan Google, yang menerima koordinat sesuai kebijakan privasi Google. Matikan baris alamat untuk menghindarinya.
  • Peta kecil pada cap: bila peta dinyalakan, aplikasi mengunduh ubin peta jalan di sekitar Anda dari OpenFreeMap (data © kontributor OpenStreetMap). Permintaan ini memperlihatkan alamat IP dan perkiraan area peta, seperti peta web pada umumnya. Matikan peta untuk menghindarinya.

Berbagi

Aplikasi tidak pernah membagikan foto sendiri. Saat Anda mengetuk Bagikan, menu Bagikan Android terbuka dan Anda yang memilih tujuannya. Foto yang dibagikan tetap membawa cap dan metadata lokasi, jadi bagikan hanya kepada orang yang Anda percaya.

Penyimpanan dan penghapusan

Foto tetap di galeri sampai Anda menghapusnya, di aplikasi atau di aplikasi galeri mana pun. Menghapus foto bercap di aplikasi ikut menghapus salinan aslinya yang bernama sama. Mencopot aplikasi menghapus pengaturannya, tetapi foto yang sudah tersimpan di galeri tetap ada. Pengembang tidak menyimpan salinan data Anda, jadi tidak ada yang perlu dimintakan penghapusan di pihak kami.

Anak-anak

Aplikasi ini alat umum dan tidak ditujukan untuk anak di bawah 13 tahun.

Perubahan
