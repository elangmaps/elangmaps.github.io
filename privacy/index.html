Elang Maps – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Elang Maps ("the app") is developed and published by Damatria Developer, an individual developer. Contact: damatria.dev@gmail.com.

Summary

Elang Maps works offline and keeps your data on your device. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Data leaves the device only when you choose to export or share it.

Data the app uses

Data Why it is used Where it is stored
Precise location (GPS) To show your position, record points, measure areas and distances, and record tracks On the device only
Location while track recording runs in the background Only after you tap Record track. A notification stays visible while recording, and recording stops when you stop it On the device only
Camera and photos Photos you take or pick for a point, optionally stamped with coordinates On the device only
Text you enter (point notes, categories, recorder name, project/client name, device code) Part of your field records and exports On the device only
Files you import (GeoPDF, SHP, KML/KMZ, GeoJSON, ZIP backups) To display maps and targets, or restore data On the device only

Network connections

  • Basemap tiles. When the device is online and an online basemap is switched on, the app requests map tiles from the map provider (currently OpenStreetMap and Esri World Imagery). Like any web request, this shares your IP address and the map area being viewed with that provider, under its own privacy policy. The app does not send your GPS position, points, or photos to it.
  • Downloading a GeoPDF from a link. If you paste a link, the app downloads the file from that address (for example Google Drive or Dropbox).
  • No other data is sent to the developer or to third parties.

Sharing

The app never shares data on its own. When you tap Export, the app creates a ZIP file on your device and opens the Android share menu. You choose where it goes, for example WhatsApp, email, or Drive.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete points or folders in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all app data from the device. Exported files you saved or shared elsewhere are not removed. Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing to request deletion of on our side.

Children

The app is a field-mapping tool for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13.

Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Protect your device with a screen lock. Exported ZIP files are not encrypted, so share them only with people you trust.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: damatria.dev@gmail.com.

Elang Maps – Kebijakan Privasi (Bahasa Indonesia)

Terakhir diperbarui: 8 November 2026

Elang Maps ("aplikasi") dikembangkan dan diterbitkan oleh Damatria Developer, pengembang perorangan. Kontak: damatria.dev@gmail.com.

Ringkasnya: Elang Maps bekerja tanpa sinyal dan menyimpan data hanya di HP Anda. Aplikasi ini tidak memakai akun, iklan, analitik, atau pelacakan. Data keluar dari HP hanya kalau Anda sendiri mengekspor atau membagikannya.

  • Lokasi akurat (GPS): dipakai untuk posisi Anda, mencatat titik, mengukur, dan merekam jalur. Datanya disimpan di HP.
  • Lokasi saat rekam jalur di latar: hanya berjalan setelah Anda mengetuk Rekam jalur. Selama perekaman ada notifikasi yang tampil, dan perekaman berhenti saat Anda menghentikannya.
  • Kamera dan foto: foto titik, bisa diberi cap koordinat. Disimpan di HP.
  • Isian teks dan berkas impor (GeoPDF, SHP, KML/KMZ, GeoJSON, ZIP): disimpan di HP.
  • Koneksi jaringan: hanya dua jenis. Pertama, ubin peta latar dari penyedia peta (saat ini OpenStreetMap dan Esri World Imagery). Penyedia menerima alamat IP dan area peta yang dilihat, bukan titik atau foto Anda. Kedua, unduhan GeoPDF dari link yang Anda masukkan.
  • Berbagi: hanya lewat tombol Ekspor, lalu Anda sendiri yang memilih tujuannya.
  • Penghapusan: hapus titik atau folder di aplikasi, hapus data aplikasi, atau copot aplikasi. Pengembang tidak menyimpan salinan data Anda.
